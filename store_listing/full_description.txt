🎂  AgeReveal — Your age, reimagined

How old are you — really? Down to the day, hour, minute, and ticking second. AgeReveal turns your birthday into a living number that grows in front of you, then enriches it with Western zodiac, Vedic Rashi, Nakshatra, Chinese animal, and lifetime heartbeat estimates.

✨ WHAT YOU GET

• Live age — years, months, days, hours, minutes, seconds (updating every second).
• Day-of-week you were born, day-of-week your next birthday falls on.
• Life milestones — celebrate your 1,000th day, 10,000th day, 25,000th day, and more.
• Vedic profile — Sidereal Rashi (Lahiri ayanamsa), one of 27 Nakshatras, Chinese zodiac cycle.
• Cosmic compatibility — enter two birthdays, get an instant Western + Chinese match score.
• Saved birthdays — family and friends, with 1-day-early reminders at a time you choose.
• Home screen widgets — 2×2 countdown + 4×2 wide widget for your top 3 upcoming birthdays.
• Shareable cards — three themes (Dark Cosmos, Minimal Light, Festive India) optimised for WhatsApp, Instagram and X.
• Optional birth time — unlock exact Nakshatra & Rashi (not approximate).
• Bilingual — English and हिन्दी (Hindi) supported.
• Dark / Light / System theme.
