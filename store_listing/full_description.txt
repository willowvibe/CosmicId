🎂 AgeReveal — Your age, reimagined

How old are you — really? Down to the day, hour, minute, and ticking second. AgeReveal turns your birthday into a living number that grows in front of you, then enriches it with Western zodiac, Vedic Rashi, Nakshatra, Chinese animal, lifetime heartbeat estimates, and a whole universe of shareable stats.

• Vedic profile — Sidereal Rashi (Lahiri ayanamsa), one of 27 Nakshatras with Pada, Chinese zodiac cycle, Stem-Branch, Tithi, Dasha, Ba Zi (Four Pillars), planetary positions.

• Saved birthdays — family and friends, with 1-day-early reminders at a time you choose.
• Home screen widgets — 2×2 seconds counter, 4×1 lifespan progress bar, 2×1 milestone ring, 4×2 birthday countdown.

• Planet ages — discover how old you'd be on Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, and more.
